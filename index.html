I am part of Sebastian Pokutta's IOL lab, deputy head of the AIS2T department at ZIB since 2022, lead of the iol.COMP group, a postdoc representative member of the MATH+ Board, and PI on several MATH+ projects.

I joined ZIB as a postdoc in 2020, following my PhD in additive and probabilistic combinatorics at UPC Barcelona under Juanjo Rué and Oriol Serra. My research develops AI methods for mathematical discovery, working at the interface of combinatorics, optimization, and machine learning.

Recent highlights include neural network-driven discovery for the Hadwiger-Nelson problem (ICML 2025, oral) and new Ramsey multiplicity bounds, the first significant progress in 27 years (FoCM 2024). Other areas of my work include flag algebras, sparse model compression (ICLR 2024), and formal proof verification.

I teach most semesters at either TUB or FUB on topics in AI, discrete mathematics, and formal proof verification. This semester I am teaching formal proof verification with Lean at FUB.
