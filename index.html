Combinatorialist working on AI for Mathematical Discovery

Christoph Spiegel

Deputy head of AIS²T at ZIB, co lead of IOL, and member of MATH+. I also teach at FUB, TUB, and BMS.

An extremal combinatorialist by training, I like to describe my approach as computational thinking: formalize problems to make them approachable through computational tools and AI.

November 2026

KolKom 2026 – Kolloquium über Kombinatorik

August 2026

TIBOR – Colloquium on Extremal Combinatorics

I jointly organized this Colloquium on Turán Numbers, Intersections, Biased Games, Orientations, and Ramsey Graphs with Leticia Mattos. Plenary talks were given by Penny Haxell, Michael Krivelevich, Anita Liebenau, Mathias Schacht, and Gábor Tardos. As the choice of topics already suggests, we used this event to celebrate Tibor Szabó’s 60th birthday.

July 2026

Graph Theory and Combinatorics Workshop at FoCM

I jointly organized this workshop with Kurusch Ebrahimi-Fard and Claudia Malvenuto at FoCM 2026 in Vienna. Talks were given by Christian Krattenthaler, Stephanie van Willigenburg, Aida Abiad, Manuel Bodirsky, Loïc Foissy, Jan Hladký, Andrey Boris Khesin, Daniel Kráľ, Svante Linusson, Hong Liu, Arnau Padrol, Oleg Pikhurko, Pascal Schweitzer, Elizabeth Xiao, Alexandra Wesolek, and Hanmeng Harmony Zhan.
